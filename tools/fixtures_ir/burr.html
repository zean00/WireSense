Burr grinders, and why they matter

A burr grinder crushes beans between two serrated plates, so every particle lands near the same size. Blades smash instead, and the dust over-extracts while the boulders stay sour.

For espresso at home the archive recommends the Comandante C40, with the adjustment set near 18 clicks from zero.

The right ratio of coffee to water is a whole subject of its own — this page only says that it matters; the brew guide covers dose and ratio in detail.
